Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as pages visited and actions taken within the Service, to help us maintain and improve it.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core Service features: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. As a teaching project, Spendly should not be relied upon to store sensitive financial information with production-grade security guarantees.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. The Service may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) solely to operate and deliver the application, and these providers only process data as necessary to provide that infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
